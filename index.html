MoneyPrinterTurbo

MoneyPrinterTurbo is a personal video tool run by Mac Tucker for the Oyster World YouTube channel, @oyst3rW0rld.

It turns a researched script into a narrated short ocean-facts video and uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and the owner is its only user.

How it uses Google data

The tool signs in to the owner's Google account with OAuth and requests two YouTube permissions: one to upload videos to the owner's channel, and one read-only permission to confirm which channel is signed in. It does not read, change, or delete anything else.
